A complete ecosystem, in one free install

Staff console, member area, public pages and a front-desk kiosk, with every module below in the same install. Native mobile apps for members are coming soon.

Inventory & lending

  • Public catalogue what you lend, with honest availability
  • Requests review, accept and track every loan
  • Front-desk handover hand out and take back at the counter
  • Evidence issue and return photos, kept private
  • QR codes and scanner boxes, tools and single units
  • Asset units drill #3, not just “one drill”
  • Containers boxes inside shelves inside rooms
  • Stock transfers between rooms and between spaces
  • Stocktake counts, gaps and discipline
  • Bulk import and QR label batches
  • Procurement the to-buy list and its receipts
  • Connected devices printers and label printers on your network

Machines

  • Machine registry every machine and who may use it
  • Certifications training records, gating and reminders
  • Service queue members ask, staff run the job
  • 3D printing print jobs, materials and intake
  • Maintenance schedules, meters and a calendar feed

Events

  • Events free, invite-only or paid
  • Tickets and promo codes
  • QR check-in at the door, even offline

Bookings

  • Rooms and resources with buffers and cutoffs
  • Recurring slots and calendar feeds

Members and accounts

  • Personal accounts one per person: sign up with a password, Google, Apple or a phone number
  • Member area each person’s own loans, returns, fees and notices
  • Membership join requests, vouching, plans and renewals
  • Mobile apps iOS and Android Coming soon

Money

  • Charges and receipts fees, deposits and late fees, always tracked
  • Online payments Stripe or Razorpay checkout

Reports and analytics

  • A report for every module loans, inventory, machines, events, bookings and more
  • Finance charges, payments and reconciliation
  • Membership analytics cohorts, retention, MRR and ARR
  • Saved views, schedules and exports CSV and Excel, delivered on time

Alerts

  • Notifications and email with quiet hours and digests
  • Chat alerts Telegram, Slack, Discord and Mattermost
  • Signed webhooks to your own systems

Running it

  • Many spaces, one server each fully separate
  • Roles you define per space, action by action
  • Audit log append-only, for every change
  • Backups archives you hold the keys to
  • Updates from GitHub releases, with a backup first
